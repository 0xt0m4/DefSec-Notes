# Threat Modelling


_It's a series of steps to improve the security of a system._


Which are:
1. Identifying what systems and applications need to be secured and what function they serve to the environment.
2. Assessing what weaknesses these infrastructures may have and how could they be exploited (penetration test exploit phase).
3. Creating a plan of action to secure these systems from the found bugs.
4. Putting in policies to prevent these bugs from occurring again (like implementing an SDLC or phishing awareness)

**IT asset**: it's a piece of software or hardware.


During these processes, many frameworks are being used, like
- `CVSS`: a scoring system to determine the importance of the bug
- `STRIDE`: some attack vectors that aren't being tested (like DoS or Spoofing)
- `DREAD`: Microsoft's assess risk system
