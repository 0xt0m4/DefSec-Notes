**Scenario**:
You bring a cup of coffee, an sit to your computer to learn.
There is always a chance you spill the coffee to the keyboard, now matter how small it is. 
The example cases can be to:

- Enjoy coffee _before_ any work happens. That would be ==risk avoidance==
- Nevertheless drinking coffee (cause you can't work without it) would be ==risk acceptance==
- Upgrading your keyboard to a spill-resistant one or getting a keyboard protector would be ==risk reduction==


Risk management is a process of: 
- identifying,
- assessing and
- responding (mitigating)
to risks associated with a particular situation || activity.


# Basic Terminology

- **Threat**: intentional || accidental event that can compromise a security infrastructure. This includes hacking, phishing attack, human error and natural disasters.
- **Vulnerability**: A software || hardware || network weakness that allows cyber-criminals to compromise a system || get unauthorized access.
- **Asset**: a resource or component the organisation relies on
- **Risk**: the probability of a threat source exploiting the system resulting in adverse business effects. 
- **Risk management**: the process of identifying, assessing and mitigating risks to maintain acceptable levels

# Methodologies

There are several frameworks for risk assessment. For instance:
- **NIST SP 800-30**
	Developed by [NIST](National Institute of Standards and Technology), involves identifying and evaluating risks, determining the likehood of impacts and developing response plan.
- **[FRAP](Facilitated Risk Analysis Process)**
	Involves a group of stakeholders working together to identify risks. Designed to be a more collaborative and inclusive approach. 
- **[OCTAVE](Operationally Critical Threat, Asset, and Vulnerability Evaluation)**
	Focuses on identifying and prioritising assets based on their criticality to the organisation's mission || assessing threats & vulnerabilities that could impact those assets.
- **[FMEA](Faliure Modes and Effect Analysis)**
	Commonly used for engineering and manufacturing. Involves identifying potential failure modes for a system || process and then analysing the possible effects for their occurrence.

**NIST SP 800-30** process entails 4 steps:
1. **Frame risk**:
	First establish the context where all risk activities occur
2. **Assess risk**:
	Identify, analyse and evaluate potential risks and their possible impacts
3. **Respond to risk**:
	Taking steps to mitigate the possibilities or impact of the risk
4. **Monitor risk**:
	Track and evaluate the effectiveness of risk responses, identify new risk and ensure the management is effective
