

This job role is basically about to review security alerts, more specifically:

- Monitor and investigate various security alerts
- Participate in SOC brainstorms and workshops
- Cooperate with other teams to keep the company safe
- Constantly learn and discover new attacks and defences




# SOC

Security Operational Center is basically the team, where SOC engineers are configuring the security tools, and analysts are helping with the attacks. The manager's job is to keep everything under control.


# Example Scenario

You found a suspicious successful SSH login: block the IP and notice the SOC team lead!
